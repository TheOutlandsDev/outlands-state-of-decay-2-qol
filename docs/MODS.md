The 16th mod PAK is the original local **2x Influence Activity Rewards** recipe, generated from the user's pinned game assets. One integration PAK makes 17 active PAKs total. Neither generated PAK is distributed.

The current list intentionally excludes the previous 2x loot-quota preset, density/AI overhauls, duplicate Daybreak team variants, and legacy first-person actor. Do not enable older conflicting presets alongside it.

The Resident Evil outfits remain, but the helicopter actor is removed from the integration metadata. The patch recipes do not change weapon or enemy combat values.
